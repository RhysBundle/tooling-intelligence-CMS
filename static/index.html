How a sequence runs

  1. 3D clipStoryline
    The approach and the physical login action, ending on a still of the device screen. One clip per login method.
  2. Web ObjectHTML
    Every screen from post-login through to log out, on one slide in one persistent Web Object. The physical acts the operator performs are shown by what the device screen displays while they happen, not by a render of the hand.
  3. 3D clipStoryline
    The closing sequence, entered when log out hands control back.

Because the middle is all screens, nothing in a sequence multiplies by product type or hardware type. A sequence costs its two bookend clips and nothing else.

What this is not

The real CMS runs on Node and stores sequences in SQLite, so an operator's work is saved and can be fetched by Storyline. None of that is in this bundle, which is a front end only.

The Storyline runtime and the variable bridge are not built either. The preview's right-hand panel is the specification for them.

Product thumbnails are placeholder line drawings. Real photographs would come from Tooling Intelligence.
